# This Land Is Our Land

Top-down alien rampage roguelite that runs in the browser. Pick one of five saucers, land in a zone, wreck enough property to hit the damage quota, beam up humans and cows for DNA, and get out before the wanted level brings in the army.

The whole game is one file, `index.html`, with no build step. Open it in a browser or deploy the folder as a static site.

## Controls

- WASD or arrows: fly
- F: auto-fire on or off (with it off, hold left click or Space)
- T: switch between auto-target and mouse aim
- E, Shift or right click (hold): abduction beam
- P or Esc: pause. M: mute
