This Land Is Our Land

Top-down alien rampage roguelite. Prototype 0.1, built for keyboard and mouse.

Pick your saucer

Land, wreck everything worth money, beam up humans and cows for DNA, and outrun the wanted level. Hit the damage quota, then fly into the green marker to extract.

Zone cleared

Pick one upgrade for the rest of the run. Keys 1 to 3 work too.

Paused

Press P or Esc to get back to it.

Shot down

Flying

  • WASD or arrows to fly
  • F toggles auto-fire. With it off, hold left click or Space
  • T switches between auto-target and aiming with the mouse
  • Hold E, Shift or right click to run the abduction beam
  • P or Esc pauses, M mutes

The run

  • Zones go farmland, suburbs, downtown, military base, then repeat hotter.
  • Quota: every wrecked building, car and cop pays out. Hit the number, then beam up.
  • Wreckage drops floating power-ups: weapons, repair, overdrive, shield, DNA and the rare Doomsday Orb.
  • Mothership: between zones pick 1 of 3 upgrades and spend DNA on repairs or rerolls.

Wanted level

  • 1 star local police
  • 2 stars SWAT vans
  • 3 stars FBI sedans
  • 4 stars army jeeps and tanks. Dodge the red target rings
  • 5 stars helicopter gunships. Bases also run SAM sites

Weapons stack up to your slot limit, and duplicates level up to 5. Fuel depots chain-explode.
